Getting started

I'm new to coaching. Where do I start?

Create an account, choose Coach, and set up your team with its age group. Your first training session will be ready to run, warm-up included. Then add your players and invite their parents.

How do I join my child's team?

Your coach will send you an invite link or an 8-letter code. Install CoachHacks, then tap the link, or tap I have an invite code in the app and type it in. Each child's invite link works for up to two parents. If it says the link has been used, ask your coach for a new one.

I joined the team but can't see my child.

If you joined with the general team link, your coach needs to link you to your child (it's one tap for them on the Parents list). Ask your coach to do that.

Signing in

I didn't get the confirmation or password-reset email.
  1. Check your spam or junk folder. Emails come from no-reply@projevity.com.au.
  2. Wait a minute, then use Send the link again (only the newest link works).
  3. Make sure there's no typo in your email address.
I tapped the confirmation link on my computer, not my phone.

That's fine: your email is confirmed. Open CoachHacks on your phone and tap I've confirmed my email: sign in.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password? and we'll email you a link. Open it on your phone to choose a new password in the app.

Privacy and your data

Who can see my child's information?

Only the coach and your child's own parents or guardians. Other parents never see it, and there are no player rankings. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my child's information?

Open the team in CoachHacks and tap Delete [name]'s data. It's deleted straight away.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. See deleting your account for what happens, or if you can't sign in.

Purchases

What do I get with CoachHacks Coach?

A one-time unlock for coaches: the full season plan, all drills and lessons, injury-prevention and strength sessions, stats and multiple teams. Parents never need to pay. Injury-prevention warm-ups are always free.

I got a new phone and lost my unlock.

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with, then go to Settings → Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested through them: reportaproblem.apple.com for iPhone, or Google Play's order history for Android.

Safety

Is the injury-prevention content medical advice?

No. It's general education based on published research, written by a physiotherapist. It isn't advice for an individual player. If a player has pain or an injury, see a doctor or physiotherapist. See the evidence behind the app.

What if I think a player has a concussion?

Remove them from play straight away, don't let them return that day, and get them checked by a medical professional. The concussion card in the app's Game Day tab walks you through it. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

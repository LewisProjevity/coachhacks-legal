Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

CoachHacks is an app for adults who coach or parent in junior football (soccer). It's made by Projevity Pty Ltd (ACN 696 573 154), Queensland, Australia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect, why, where it's kept, how long we keep it, and how to delete it. We've written it in plain English. If anything's unclear, email support@projevity.com.au.

The short version

  • CoachHacks is for adults. Children never use the app and never have accounts.
  • Coaches and parents enter only limited information about children: a first name and last initial, and team details like minutes played. Never a date of birth, a photo, contact details or medical information.
  • A parent sees only their own child's information. Other parents never see it.
  • We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use tracking or analytics tools.
  • Your data is stored in Sydney, Australia.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time (Settings → Delete account), and parents can delete their child's information at any time.

1. Who this policy covers

It covers everyone who uses the CoachHacks app or this website: coaches, who create and run teams, and parents and guardians, who join a team when a coach invites them. It also covers the information coaches and parents enter about the children on their team.

2. Information we collect

About you (coaches and parents)

  • Account details: your email address and, if you choose to give it, your first name. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address and name they share with us. Apple lets you hide your email, in which case we get a private relay address.
  • Sign-in information: a securely hashed password (if you use email sign-in; we never see your password itself) or the account identifier from Apple or Google.
  • Your settings: your country, whether you say "soccer" or "football", and your coaching preferences (for example, fun versus winning), which the app uses to tailor its tips.
  • Your activity in the app: the date you last used CoachHacks (so we can remove inactive accounts) and, for coaches, private notes such as post-game reflections. Only you can see those.
  • Purchases: whether you've unlocked CoachHacks Coach. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a device token so we can send reminders. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

About children on a team

Coaches and parents may enter:

  • first name and last initial (never a full surname)
  • an optional shirt number
  • position preferences, and whether a child doesn't want to play in goal
  • availability for training and games
  • minutes played, and match stats (goals, assists, clean sheets)
  • strengths and "working on" areas chosen by the coach from a fixed list

We don't collect children's dates of birth (the team's age group is used instead), photos, contact details, or any injury or medical information. Children don't use CoachHacks and can't create accounts.

About teams

Team name and age group, schedule (dates, times and locations of training and games), results and ladder positions, and announcements from the coach to parents.

Technical information

If the app crashes, it sends us a crash report: the app version, your phone's model and operating system, and where in our code the error happened. Before a report leaves your phone, the app removes names, email addresses, sign-in tokens and anything you typed. We don't use advertising identifiers.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide the app: create your account, run your team, show parents the schedule and their own child's information, and send the emails you need (confirming your email, resetting your password, team invitations and important team notices).
  • To keep it safe and working: preventing misuse, and fixing crashes and bugs.
  • To give you what you paid for: recording whether you've unlocked CoachHacks Coach.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We don't use your information, or children's information, for advertising, profiling or selling.

Legal bases (GDPR and UK GDPR): providing the service you asked for (contract); our legitimate interests in running a safe, working app and in team management for the coach and families involved; your consent for push notifications; and legal obligations.

4. Who can see what

  • The coach can see all the team's information, including every child on the team.
  • A parent can see the team's schedule and announcements, and only their own child's information. A coach's notes on strengths and "working on" areas are shared with that child's parents only if the coach chooses to share them.
  • Other parents never see your child's information, and there are no player rankings or leaderboards.
  • Projevity staff access data only when needed to provide support, fix problems or meet legal obligations.

These rules are enforced by the database itself, not just by the app's screens.

5. Service providers we use

We share information only with the companies that run parts of CoachHacks for us. They may use it only to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in system and server functions. All app data is stored on Amazon Web Services in Sydney, Australia.
  • Sentry: crash reports, stored in the European Union (Frankfurt), with personal details removed as described above. Sentry is set not to store IP addresses.
  • Resend: sends our emails (for example, email confirmation and password resets). It receives your email address and the message.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in if you choose "Continue with Apple" or "Continue with Google", app distribution, in-app purchases and push notifications.
  • RevenueCat: checks in-app purchase receipts so your unlock works on all your devices. It receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase records, not your name or email.
  • GitHub: hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety. If Projevity is ever sold or merged, this policy would continue to apply to your information.

6. Where it's stored and how long we keep it

App data is stored in Sydney, Australia. Crash reports are stored in the EU. Some providers (Resend, Apple, Google and GitHub) are based in the United States and may process information there. Where information goes overseas we use providers with contractual privacy protections (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

  • Your account: until you delete it, or until it's been inactive for 2 years. We'll email you 30 days before deleting an inactive account, so you can keep it by opening the app.
  • A team's information (including the children on it): until the coach deletes the team, or the coach's account is deleted without handing the team to another parent.
  • A child's information: until a parent or the coach deletes it, or the team is deleted.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Backups: our database is backed up daily, and backups are kept for 7 days. Deleted information disappears from backups within 7 days of deletion.
  • Email delivery logs at Resend: kept for a short period under Resend's retention settings, then deleted.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account. It's permanent and immediate. If you coach a team, the app first asks you to hand it to a parent on the team or delete it. You can also email us to delete it; see how to delete your account.
  • Delete your child's information: open the team, then tap "Delete [name]'s data".
  • Leave a team at any time from the team screen.
  • Access, correct or get a copy of your information: you can see and edit most of it in the app, or email us and we'll respond within 30 days.
  • Object or complain: email us first and we'll try to fix it. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, or your local data protection authority if you're in the EU or UK.

We'll check it's really you before acting on a request about your information.

8. Children

CoachHacks is made for adults, and children don't use it. We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Coaches should only add children whose parents, guardians or club have agreed to them being added, and we keep the information about children to the minimum a coach needs to run a fair, safe team. If you think a child's information has been added without permission, email us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is controlled by database rules that are automatically tested to make sure parents can't see other families' information. If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll notify you and the regulator as the law requires.

10. California residents

We collect the categories of information described in section 2 for the purposes in section 3. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we don't use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics. You can ask to know, correct or delete your information by emailing us, and we won't treat you differently for doing so.

11. Changes

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.

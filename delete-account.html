Delete your CoachHacks account

You can delete your CoachHacks account, made by Projevity Pty Ltd, and the data linked to it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open CoachHacks and go to Team → Settings (or the gear icon on the Home screen).
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. If you coach a team, choose whether to hand it to a parent on the team or delete the team. If you delete it, its parents are told by email.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account. If you signed in with Apple, you'll be asked to confirm with your Apple ID.

Your account is deleted straight away.

By email (if you can't use the app)

Email support@projevity.com.au from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my CoachHacks account". We'll confirm it's you, then delete your account within 7 days and email you when it's done.

What's deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details, profile and settings.
  • Your memberships of teams, and your links to your children.
  • For coaches: any team you delete, including the children's information on it.

What's kept, and for how long

  • Database backups keep deleted information for up to 7 days, then it's gone.
  • Crash reports, which don't contain your name or email, are deleted within 90 days.
  • A team you hand to another parent stays with them, along with its players' information.
  • Your purchase history is kept by Apple or Google under their own policies. We don't hold payment records.

Only want to remove your child's information?

You don't need to delete your account. Open the team in the app and tap Delete [name]'s data.
